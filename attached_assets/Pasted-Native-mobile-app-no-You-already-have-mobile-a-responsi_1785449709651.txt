Native mobile app — no. You already have mobile: a responsive web page opens fine on a phone, and a link is easier to send than an app store listing. Native means app store review, a separate build, Apple's and Google's rules about financial apps, and ongoing maintenance of a second codebase — for six clients who will most likely open this on a laptop while sitting with you anyway. This is the suggestion that costs the most and returns the least.

Add the logo — yes, but it was already in the spec. That's not a new feature, it's an unfinished line item. Upload the image, tell it the filename, and make sure it lands in both places: the page header and the PDF. Worth checking the PDF specifically, since jsPDF handles images differently than the browser does and it's a common spot for it to silently come out blank or stretched.

Advisor-adjustable scoring weights — no, and this is the one I'd actively decline. It sounds like flexibility and it's really a compliance liability. The moment the weights are dialable from a settings panel, the score stops being a framework and becomes an output you can steer. If a client scores 34 and someone nudges the longevity weight until it reads 61, the number no longer means anything — and the paper trail shows an advisor tuning a model until it justified a sale. That's exactly the dynamic your whole critique of the original article was aimed at.

Keep the weights in code, commented, where changing them is a deliberate versioned edit you can point to and explain. If you decide the flexibility component should be worth 30 instead of 25, that's a considered methodology change, not a slider.

Worth noting generally: Replit's suggestion panel is built to propose more work, and "more work" and "better tool" aren't the same thing here. The strength of this build is that it's small, deterministic, and does one thing clearly. Most additions will erode that. The finish line is closer than the panel implies — logo in, real payout rates swapped for the placeholders, compliance read on the copy, deploy.
